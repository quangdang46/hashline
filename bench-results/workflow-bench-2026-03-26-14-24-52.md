## Assessment

- This workflow benchmark focuses on end-to-end task outcomes, not just primitive throughput.
- Safe stale/ambiguity rejections are tracked separately from unsafe wrong edits so correctness is not collapsed into raw speed.
- Command count and estimated tokens processed help show whether a workflow is cheap to drive, not merely fast in isolation.
