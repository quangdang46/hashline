## hashline vs sed (str-replace) comparison

> hashline adds content-hash safety (stale anchor detection, atomic writes) at comparable raw speed to sed. sed is a pure text-stream tool with no safety checks. Fixture line 50000.

### Single-line replace on large.rs (100k L)

| Tool | Mean | Range |
|------|-----:|------:|
| hashline patch SWAP \| large.rs | 121.5 ms | 106.8–135.1 ms |
| sed -i line replace \| large.rs | 64.8 ms | 58.2–69.0 ms |

## Notes

- Anchors are content hashes: edits stay valid when unrelated lines move; stale anchors fail loudly instead of editing the wrong line.
- `--safe` (atomic temp+rename) is slower than the default write path and is not benched here; use it when crash-safety matters more than throughput.
